# Cybersecurity Analyst Lab Portfolio
Welcome! 

This repository documents the hands-on labs and projects I completed throughout the Per Scholas Cybersecurity Analyst Program, an intensive 15-week cybersecurity training program focused on developing practical skills in networking, security operations, threat detection, vulnerability management, and incident response.

I'm using this repository to document my progress, reinforce what I learn, and demonstrate my ability to apply cybersecurity concepts in hands-on environments.

### About This Repository

Rather than simply documenting completed coursework, this repository focuses on what I configured, analyzed, investigated, and learned during each lab.

Lab documentation may include:

- Splunk searches, alerts, and dashboards
- Incident investigation notes

- Screenshots and supporting evidence
- Key technical takeaways

## Lab Documentation

Each lab folder is designed to show both the **technical process** and my understanding of the concepts being practiced.

Where appropriate, individual lab documentation includes: 

### Objective

What I was trying to configure, investigate, analyze, or troubleshoot.

### Environment & Tools

The systems, applications, commands, and tools used during the lab.

### Technical Process

The configuration or investigation process I followed.

### Verification

How I confirmed that the configuration worked or validated my findings.

Examples may include:

- ping
- ipconfig
- arp -a
- netstat
- tracert
- nslookup

### Key Takeaways

What I learned from this lab and how the concepts relate to real-world networking or cybersecurity operations.

### Troubleshooting

Problems encountered during the lab, how I investigated them, and how they were resolved.
